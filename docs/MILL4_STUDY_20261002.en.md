## Assumptions and structure

Concept assumptions, not manufacturer specifications: vertical XYZ+C machine, X500 (±250), Y300 (±150), Z300 (±150) mm and C±360°. Bed 900×700, column height900, table700×240, C rotorØ200, cube100 and toolØ20 mm. Assumed BT30/8000 rpm/3.7 kW spindle, 50 kg payload, 10 m/min rapid, 500 mm/min study feed and 30°/s indexing. Spindle rotation, cutting, NC and TCP are outside these four-axis charts.

The initial nine-part flat concept was `output/Mill4-20261002-1328-concept/concept.asm`; MCP/add-in connection and XYZC import worked. Tool/workpiece spacing, head attachment and C support needed detail.

## Study motions

Linear interpolation is not a servo profile with acceleration/jerk. Coordinates are mm/degrees:

- 16 s endpoints, every4 s: (0,0,150,0)→(−250,−150,150,0)→(250,150,150,0)→(0,0,−150,0)→(0,0,150,0). Limit exploration, not a cleared operating path.
- 24 s index/retract: retract Z150, C90, descend Z−100, retract, then C180/270/360 every4 s. Detailed-model descent was changed to Z0 to preserve 20 mm gap.
- 16 s simultaneous dry run every4 s: (0,0,150,0)→(100,50,100,90)→(0,100,50,180)→(−100,50,100,270)→(0,0,150,360). Not NC machining.
- Detailed contact-height motion: Z150→0→−20→150, indexing C90 only while retracted. Use interference stop for unverified motions.
