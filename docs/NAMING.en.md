# Product name and related products

Decision dated 2026-09-30: **MechCue**, pronounced “Meck Cue.” “Mech” refers to mechanisms and “Cue” to a timed instruction, also fitting possible future PLC integration.

| Product | Related area |
|---|---|
| [Solid Edge](https://solidedge.siemens.com/en/solutions/products/3d-design/assembly-modeling-and-management/) | Motors, timeline and animation |
| [Visual Components](https://www.visualcomponents.com/products/robot-offline-programming/) | 3D production simulation and PLC/robot connections |
| [Emulate3D](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/products/software/factorytalk/designsuite/emulate3d-digital-twin.html) | Digital twins and virtual commissioning |

These are related products, not equivalents. MechCue concentrates on direct displacement-chart editing and assignment to Solid Edge. The naming study did not position it as a substitute for PLC-connected virtual commissioning with physics.

The study's general web searches for `"MechCue"`, `"MechCue" software CAD simulation`, `"MechCue" GitHub` and its Japanese spelling found no same-named software. KineTrace and MotionLoom were rejected because existing software used those names. This was a limited search, not trademark clearance or a check of unpublished products/domain availability. Descriptions must not imply that MechCue is an official Siemens product.
